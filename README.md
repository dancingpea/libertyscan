# Libertyscan

Which of your iPhone and Mac apps could you still update and reinstall after moving your Apple Account to another country?

Libertyscan reads the App Store history in Apple's data export and checks every app against the App Stores of France, Germany, Japan, the Netherlands, the United Kingdom and the United States. It shows what you'd lose, ranks the countries, and gives you a checklist for the switch. Free, and your Apple file is read on your device, never uploaded.

This repository holds the published page only (`index.html`, one self-contained file). Open the site from the link beside *About*.
